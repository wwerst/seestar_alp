Both share the telescope's wifi uplink, so a rising RTT next to heavy video bytes/s is the fingerprint of the uplink saturating and delaying mount commands. Reusable: include on any page that has a telescope_id in context, e.g. {% include 'partials/net_stats.html' %} It polls /api/{id}/netstats with a plain fetch (no HTMX swap, so no destructive empty-swap risk) at a bounded, slow cadence and updates the readout text in place, leaving the last values untouched on a failed poll. #}
